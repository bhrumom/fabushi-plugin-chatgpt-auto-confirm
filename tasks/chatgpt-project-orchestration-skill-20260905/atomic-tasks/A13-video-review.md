# A13 — Video cross-review

- **Status:** `planned`
- **Goal:** Have the review group independently verify the full test video and evidence lineage.
- **Owns:** video review record and result rubric
- **Depends on:** A09, A12
- **Produces:** `VIDEO-REVIEW-PASS` or actionable rejection
- **Acceptance:** reviewer confirms exact main SHA/version/run, real user actions, expected results, and correspondence of video/screenshots/trace/report/logs; missing or stale evidence blocks.
- **Blocked by:** accessible complete video artifact
